# 📚 LMS Quick Summary - One Page Overview

## What is This System?
A **Learning Management System** - like a digital school for your company where employees take courses, earn certificates, and track their learning progress.

---

## 👥 Four Types of Users

| Role | Who | What They Do | Key Powers |
|------|-----|--------------|------------|
| 🔴 **Super Admin** | Company owner | Control everything | Full system access |
| 🟠 **HR Admin** | HR staff | Manage employees | Assign training, view reports |
| 🟢 **Instructor** | Trainers/Teachers | Create courses | Build content, grade tests |
| 🔵 **Employee** | Regular staff | Learn | Take courses, earn certificates |

---

## 🔄 Complete Flow in 5 Steps

### 1️⃣ SETUP
- Admin creates departments and users
- Instructor creates courses with modules and lessons
- Instructor uploads videos, PDFs, and materials

### 2️⃣ ENROLLMENT
- Employee browses catalog and enrolls
- OR Admin assigns mandatory courses
- OR AI recommends relevant courses

### 3️⃣ LEARNING
- Employee watches videos and reads materials
- System tracks progress automatically
- Can pause and resume anytime

### 4️⃣ ASSESSMENT
- Employee takes quizzes/tests
- AI automatically grades (even essays!)
- Instructor reviews flagged answers

### 5️⃣ COMPLETION
- Course completed → Certificate generated automatically
- Employee downloads certificate
- Can verify certificate with QR code
