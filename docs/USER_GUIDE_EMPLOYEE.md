## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the LMS URL provided by your organization
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. If 2FA is enabled, enter the verification code from your authenticator app

### First-Time Setup

After your first login:

1. Complete your profile by clicking on your avatar in the top-right corner
2. Upload a profile picture (optional)
3. Add a bio and update your preferences
4. Set your notification preferences

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard is your home base in the LMS. It displays:

- **My Courses**: Courses you're currently enrolled in
- **Recommended Courses**: AI-powered course recommendations based on your profile and learning history
- **Recent Activity**: Your latest learning activities
- **Upcoming Deadlines**: Mandatory courses and assessments due soon
- **Progress Summary**: Overall learning statistics
- **Achievements**: Recently earned certificates

### Navigation

Use the sidebar to access:

- **Dashboard**: Return to your home page
- **Course Catalog**: Browse all available courses
- **My Courses**: View your enrolled courses
- **My Learning Path**: View your personalized learning path
- **Certificates**: Access your earned certificates
- **Profile**: Manage your account settings

## Browsing Courses

### Course Catalog

1. Click "Course Catalog" in the sidebar
2. Browse courses by:
   - **Categories**: Filter by subject area
   - **Difficulty Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
   - **Duration**: Short, Medium, Long courses
   - **Tags**: Specific skills or topics

### Search

Use the search bar to find specific courses:

1. Enter keywords related to the course topic
2. Results update in real-time
3. Click on a course card to view details

### Course Details

Each course page shows:

- Course title and description
- Learning objectives
- Prerequisites (if any)
- Estimated duration
- Difficulty level
- Instructor information
- Course outline (modules and lessons)
- Reviews and ratings

## Enrolling in Courses

### Self-Enrollment

1. Navigate to the course detail page
2. Review prerequisites (you must complete these first)
3. Click "Enroll Now"
4. Confirm your enrollment
5. The course appears in "My Courses"

### Mandatory Courses

Some courses are automatically assigned by your department or HR:

- These appear in your dashboard with a "Mandatory" badge
- Check the deadline in the course card
- Complete these courses by the specified date

### Prerequisites

If a course has prerequisites:

- You must complete prerequisite courses first
- The "Enroll" button will be disabled until prerequisites are met
- Hover over the button to see which prerequisites are required

## Taking Courses

### Accessing Course Content

1. Go to "My Courses"
2. Click on the course you want to continue
3. The course player opens with the lesson navigation sidebar

### Lesson Types

The LMS supports multiple content types:

#### Video Lessons

- Click play to start the video
- Use playback controls (play, pause, volume, quality)
- The system automatically bookmarks your position
- You can skip forward/backward using the timeline

#### PDF Documents

- View PDFs directly in the browser
- Use zoom controls to adjust size
- Navigate pages using the sidebar or arrow keys
- Add annotations (highlights, notes) if enabled

#### Text Lessons

- Read content directly on the page
- Scroll through the material
- Click "Mark as Complete" when finished

#### SCORM Content

- Interactive content loads in the player
- Follow on-screen instructions
- Progress is tracked automatically

### Progress Tracking

The system automatically tracks:

- Time spent on each lesson
- Completion status
- Bookmarks (where you left off)
- Overall course progress percentage

### Lesson Navigation

Use the sidebar to:

- View all modules and lessons
- See completion status (checkmarks)
- Jump to any lesson
- Track your progress through the course

### Completing Lessons

1. Finish watching/reading the lesson content
2. Click "Mark as Complete" (if not auto-completed)
3. Click "Next Lesson" to continue
4. Your progress updates automatically

## Taking Assessments

### Starting an Assessment

1. Navigate to the assessment from the course page
2. Read the instructions carefully
3. Note the time limit and passing score
4. Click "Start Assessment"
5. The timer begins immediately

### Question Types

You may encounter:

- **Multiple Choice**: Select one correct answer
- **Multiple Select**: Choose all correct answers
- **True/False**: Select true or false
- **Fill in the Blank**: Type the correct answer
- **Essay**: Write a detailed response
- **Matching**: Match items from two columns
- **Drag and Drop**: Arrange items in correct order

### During the Assessment

- The timer displays at the top of the screen
- Answer questions in any order
- Use "Flag for Review" to mark questions
- Click "Save" to save your progress
- You can navigate between questions using the question list

### Submitting

1. Review your answers
2. Check for any unanswered questions (highlighted in red)
3. Click "Submit Assessment"
4. Confirm your submission
5. You cannot change answers after submission

### Auto-Submit

If time expires:

- The assessment auto-submits automatically
- All answered questions are saved
- Unanswered questions are marked as incorrect

### Viewing Results

After submission:

- Your score displays immediately (for objective questions)
- Essay questions may take longer (AI grading + instructor review)
- View correct answers (if enabled by instructor)
- Read feedback and explanations
- See which questions you got wrong

### Retaking Assessments

If allowed:

- Check the number of attempts remaining
- Review your previous attempt before retrying
- Study the feedback and correct answers
- Click "Retake Assessment" when ready

### My Learning Dashboard

Access detailed analytics:

- Total courses completed

The AI analyzes your:

- Current role and department
- Completed courses
- Assessment performance
- Career goals
- Skill gaps

**Using Recommendations:**

1. View recommendations on your dashboard
2. Read the AI's reasoning for each suggestion
3. Click "View Course" to see details

Get a custom learning roadmap:

1. Click "My Learning Path" in the sidebar
2. Click "Generate New Path" (if you don't have one)
3. Select your target role or skill

5. Follow the path to achieve your learning goals

**Learning Path Features:**

- Courses ordered by prerequisites and difficulty
- Estimated total duration

   - Finding courses

   - Enrollment assistance
   - Answering course-related questions

## Certificates

### Earning Certificates

You receive a certificate when:

- You complete all course lessons
- You pass all required assessments
- You meet the minimum passing score

### Accessing Certificates

1. Go to "Certificates" in the sidebar
2. View all your earned certificates
3. Click on a certificate to view details

### Certificate Features

Each certificate includes:

- Your name and course title
- Completion date
- Unique certificate number
- QR code for verification
- Instructor signature (digital)

### Downloading Certificates

1. Open the certificate
2. Click "Download PDF"
3. Save to your device
4. Share on LinkedIn or with employers

### Sharing Certificates

- Click "Share" to get a public verification link
- Add to your LinkedIn profile
- Include in your resume or portfolio
- Anyone can verify authenticity using the QR code

### Certificate Verification

To verify a certificate:

1. Go to the verification page
2. Enter the certificate number or scan QR code
3. View certificate details and authenticity status

## Tips for Success

### Best Practices

- **Set a Schedule**: Dedicate specific times for learning
- **Complete Mandatory Courses First**: Avoid missing deadlines
- **Take Notes**: Use the annotation features
- **Review Before Assessments**: Study the material thoroughly
- **Use Recommendations**: Explore AI-suggested courses
- **Track Progress**: Monitor your completion rates
- **Ask Questions**: Use the chatbot or contact instructors

### Keyboard Shortcuts

Speed up your learning:

- `Space`: Play/Pause video
- `→`: Skip forward 10 seconds
- `←`: Skip backward 10 seconds
- `F`: Toggle fullscreen
- `M`: Mute/Unmute
- `Esc`: Exit fullscreen

### Mobile Learning

Access courses on mobile devices:

- Responsive design works on all screen sizes
- Download lessons for offline viewing (if enabled)
- Use the mobile app (if available)
- Sync progress across devices

### Accessibility Features

The LMS supports:

- Screen readers
- Keyboard navigation
- Closed captions on videos
- Adjustable text size
- High contrast mode
- Dark mode

To enable accessibility features:

1. Go to Profile > Preferences
2. Enable desired accessibility options
3. Adjust settings to your needs

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**

A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset instructions.

**Q: Can I download course content?**

A: Only if the instructor has enabled downloads for that course.

**Q: How long do I have to complete a course?**

A: Self-paced courses have no deadline unless they're mandatory. Check the course details for specific deadlines.

**Q: What happens if I fail an assessment?**

A: You can retake it if attempts are remaining. Review the feedback and study before retrying.

**Q: Can I pause a video and resume later?**

A: Yes, the system automatically bookmarks your position.

**Q: How do I know which courses are mandatory?**

A: Mandatory courses have a "Mandatory" badge and appear in your dashboard with deadlines.

**Q: Can I suggest new courses?**

A: Yes, use the feedback form to suggest courses you'd like to see.

**Q: Are certificates recognized externally?**

A: Certificates are issued by your organization. Recognition depends on your industry and employer.

**Q: How is my data protected?**

A: We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. See the Privacy Policy for details.

**Q: Can I learn offline?**

A: Some content may be downloadable for offline access if enabled by your administrator.
